FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Get answers to the main questions about the method and start your journey into the new era of design.

01Do I need to know how to code or have design experience?

No. You learn from scratch, without typing a single line of code. The AI Agents handle the technical side, and the course teaches you the design that makes a page look premium.

02How much will I spend on AI tools?

You can do it all for free: Google Antigravity to create the pages, Vercel to publish them and Google Flow for the videos. If you already pay for Claude, Codex or Lovable, the same process works there too. In the course we show you the options and you pick.

03Do I need a powerful computer?

No. The AI runs in the cloud, so any Windows or Mac computer with a stable internet connection will do. You just can't do it on your phone.

04How many lessons are there?

There are 38 lessons, almost 12 hours of content, from zero to a published page. Every lesson has English audio and subtitles, and the student area is in English too. And new lessons keep being added to the course.

05Will I be able to create pages like the ones on this page?

Yes. The pages you see here and in our ads were made with the course method: complete, animated and working on mobile too. In the course you create pages like these from scratch, step by step.

06Does the course teach me how to put the page live?

Yes, from start to finish. You can publish for free on Vercel or on the hosting you already use, like Hostinger, with your client's domain. We don't use WordPress or Elementor: you make changes just by asking the AI. And the page stays live even after your access to the course ends.

07Does the course teach me how to get clients?

Yes. Modules 05 and 06 teach you how to put together a premium portfolio, even with no clients, how to price your work and how to prospect to close High-Ticket projects.

08Is there support for my questions?

Yes, you'll get access to our VIP support and our exclusive student community to share experiences and get your questions answered. If you want a refund within the 7-day guarantee, just request it through Hotmart support.
